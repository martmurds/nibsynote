NibsyNote is a paper-style notebook with a built-in productivity coach. Write, plan and reflect on warm, hand-crafted pages — and let Nibsy quietly nudge you when a task keeps sliding to tomorrow.

18 paper templates

Apple Pencil ready

Type, sketch, annotate and handwrite straight onto the page.

Nibsy, your gentle coach

Spots tasks you keep putting off and offers to shrink, delegate or let them go.

Daily & Weekly Review

Set priorities, schedule your day, and reflect on what mattered.

Time Travel

Scrub back through earlier versions of any note, anytime.

iCloud sync

Your notes stay in step across iPhone and iPad, privately.

Private by design

Your notes are stored in your own iCloud account. No tracking, no ads and no third-party analytics inside your notebook.
